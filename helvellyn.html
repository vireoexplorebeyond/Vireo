Helvellyn via Striding Edge

One of the Lake District's classic big days: a high-level approach, an exposed ridge and a huge summit panorama.

13 kmApprox. distance
5–6 hrsTypical duration
~950mElevation gain
HardExperience recommended

The Vireo take

Striding Edge is the reason to choose this route. It is narrow and exposed in places, with scrambling and steep drops beside the ridge. In poor weather it can feel dramatically different from a calm summer day.

This is a planning prototype, not a safety briefing. Conditions in the mountains change quickly. Check an appropriate weather forecast, access information and a detailed map before setting out, and choose a route within your experience.
